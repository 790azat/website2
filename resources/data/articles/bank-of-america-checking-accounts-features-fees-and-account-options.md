Bank of America offers several checking account options through its Advantage Banking lineup, with differences in monthly maintenance fees, minimum-balance requirements, overdraft features, interest, and access to additional banking services.

For consumers comparing checking accounts, the monthly fee is only one part of the decision. The way a fee can be waived, whether the account supports paper checks, how overdrafts are handled, access to digital banking tools, and eligibility for relationship-based benefits can all affect the account's overall cost.

Bank of America's current consumer checking lineup includes **Advantage SafeBalance Banking, Advantage Plus Banking, and Advantage Relationship Banking**, along with specialized options for younger customers and families.

## Bank of America Checking Account Options

The three primary Advantage Banking accounts serve different purposes.

| Account | Monthly Maintenance Fee | Key Fee-Waiver Requirement | Notable Features |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Advantage SafeBalance Banking | $4.95 | $500 minimum daily balance, owner under 25, or qualifying BofA Rewards status | No overdraft item fees; digital-focused banking |
| Advantage Plus Banking | $12 | $1,500 minimum daily balance, qualifying $250+ direct deposit, or qualifying BofA Rewards status | Flexible everyday checking; Balance Connect |
| Advantage Relationship Banking | $25 | $20,000 minimum daily balance or qualifying BofA Rewards status | Interest-bearing account; additional deposit benefits |

These fees and waiver requirements come from Bank of America's current account disclosures.

The appropriate account depends partly on how much money normally remains in the account and whether the customer receives direct deposits.

## Advantage SafeBalance Banking

Advantage SafeBalance Banking is designed around relatively simple everyday banking.

The account currently has a **$4.95 monthly maintenance fee**, but Bank of America waives the fee when at least one qualifying condition is met during the statement cycle.

Those conditions include maintaining a minimum daily balance of $500, having an account owner under age 25, or meeting applicable BofA Rewards requirements.

One significant feature is the absence of Overdraft Item Fees. Bank of America describes SafeBalance as a digital-focused account without paper check writing, and the account is designed to help customers avoid spending more than their available funds.

This structure can be relevant for customers who primarily use a debit card, mobile banking, electronic payments, and direct deposits rather than writing checks.

### SafeBalance and overdrafts

SafeBalance differs from some traditional checking accounts because Bank of America does not charge Overdraft Item Fees on this account.

The bank may instead decline a transaction when there are insufficient available funds. This means customers should still monitor their available balance because a declined payment can potentially result in a fee or other consequence from the merchant or biller.

## Advantage Plus Banking

Advantage Plus Banking is positioned as a more flexible everyday checking account.

Its standard monthly maintenance fee is **$12**. Bank of America lists several ways to have the fee waived:

* Maintain a minimum daily balance of at least $1,500
* Receive at least one qualifying direct deposit of $250 or more
* Meet applicable BofA Rewards requirements

The direct-deposit waiver can make the account particularly relevant for customers who receive a regular paycheck or other qualifying electronic deposits.

Unlike SafeBalance, Advantage Plus supports a broader range of payment options and offers access to **Balance Connect for overdraft protection**.

Balance Connect can automatically transfer available funds from eligible linked Bank of America accounts when needed. Bank of America states that it does not charge a transfer fee for Balance Connect, although interest or other charges can apply when certain credit accounts are used as backup sources.

## Advantage Relationship Banking

Advantage Relationship Banking is designed for customers who maintain larger balances or have a broader relationship with Bank of America.

The account has a **$25 monthly maintenance fee** and requires a minimum of $100 to open. Bank of America describes it as an interest-bearing, variable-rate checking account.

The $25 monthly fee can generally be waived by maintaining a minimum daily balance of $20,000 or by meeting applicable BofA Rewards requirements.

The $20,000 requirement is a **minimum daily balance**, meaning the account must remain at or above that threshold during the applicable statement cycle to satisfy the waiver condition.

The account also provides additional deposit-service benefits, including no fee for incoming domestic wire transfers according to Bank of America's schedule of fees.

For customers who maintain substantial cash balances, the account's interest feature and relationship benefits may therefore be more relevant than the monthly fee alone.

## Checking Accounts for Children, Students, and Young Adults

Bank of America also offers banking options designed for younger customers.

Advantage SafeBalance Banking can be used by students and young adults, while **Advantage SafeBalance Banking for Family Banking** is a parent-owned account designed to provide younger children with a debit card and digital banking access alongside parental controls.

Bank of America states that qualifying accounts for children, teens, and young adults can have no monthly maintenance fee until age 25, subject to the applicable account terms.

The Family Banking account is structured differently from a standard adult checking account and does not offer some features, including direct deposit and Zelle, according to Bank of America's current product information.

## Bank of America Checking Fees

Monthly maintenance fees are only one category of checking-account costs.

Depending on the account and transaction, consumers may also encounter fees associated with:

* Out-of-network ATM use
* International transactions
* Stop-payment requests
* Wire transfers
* Certain paper checks or check services
* Other account services

Bank of America publishes a personal schedule of fees that should be reviewed before opening an account because fee amounts and waiver conditions can change.

The bank's account disclosures also distinguish between services that are included with a particular account and services that may carry separate charges.

## Overdraft Protection and Balance Connect

Overdraft handling is an important difference between Bank of America's checking options.

Balance Connect for overdraft protection allows eligible checking accounts to be linked to up to five eligible Bank of America backup accounts. If available funds are insufficient for a transaction, the service can automatically transfer funds from an eligible backup account.

There is no transfer fee for Balance Connect itself. However, if a credit card, line of credit, or another credit-based account is used as the funding source, interest or other charges can apply according to that account's terms.

SafeBalance is different because it does not charge Overdraft Item Fees and is not eligible for Balance Connect as either a covered or linked backup account.

## BofA Rewards and Checking Accounts

Bank of America's relationship program changed in 2026. The former Preferred Rewards program transitioned to **BofA Rewards** beginning May 27, 2026.

Bank of America states that eligible personal checking customers can enroll in BofA Rewards without a minimum balance requirement. Higher qualifying tiers can provide additional banking and investment-related benefits, including fee-waiver eligibility for certain accounts.

This means the effective cost of a Bank of America checking account can depend on more than the checking balance itself. Customers with qualifying Bank of America and Merrill relationships may have access to benefits that change the economics of maintaining the account.

## Digital Banking Features

Bank of America checking accounts provide access to online and mobile banking tools.

The bank offers mobile check deposit, account alerts, digital-wallet functionality, debit-card controls, and other account-management features. Customers can also temporarily lock a misplaced debit card through digital banking tools.

For customers who rarely visit a financial center, these tools can be particularly relevant when comparing account convenience.

## Direct Deposit and Checking Account Fees

Direct deposit can have an important role in determining the cost of an Advantage Plus account.

A qualifying direct deposit of at least $250 during the statement cycle can satisfy one of the requirements for waiving the account's $12 monthly maintenance fee.

For someone receiving a regular paycheck, this can eliminate the monthly charge without requiring a $1,500 minimum balance.

Consumers should verify whether a particular deposit qualifies under the bank's definition of direct deposit rather than assuming that every electronic deposit will satisfy the requirement.

## Bank of America Checking Accounts and FDIC Insurance

Eligible deposits held at Bank of America are generally covered by FDIC insurance within applicable limits.

The standard FDIC insurance limit is **$250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category**.

The insurance applies to qualifying deposit accounts, not every financial product offered by a financial institution. Consumers with substantial balances should therefore understand how account ownership categories affect available coverage.

## How to Choose Between Bank of America Checking Accounts

The monthly fee and waiver requirement provide a useful starting point.

A customer comparing the accounts can consider several scenarios:

**Lower-balance everyday banking:** SafeBalance may have a relatively straightforward structure, particularly for customers who qualify for the fee waiver or want an account without Overdraft Item Fees.

**Regular direct deposit:** Advantage Plus provides a $12 monthly fee waiver for a qualifying $250 or greater direct deposit, subject to the account terms.

**Larger balances:** Advantage Relationship requires a substantially higher balance to waive its monthly fee, but provides an interest-bearing checking account and additional relationship features.

**Younger customers:** Age-based fee waivers and specialized family/student options may change which account is appropriate.

## Questions to Ask Before Opening an Account

Before opening a Bank of America checking account, consider:

1. What is the monthly maintenance fee?
2. How can the fee be waived?
3. Does my direct deposit qualify?
4. Is there a minimum daily balance requirement?
5. Does the account earn interest?
6. Are Overdraft Item Fees charged?
7. Is Balance Connect available?
8. What ATM fees could apply?
9. What wire-transfer fees apply?
10. Are there age-based fee waivers?
11. Can I qualify for BofA Rewards benefits?
12. What happens if my balance falls below a fee-waiver threshold?

## The Bottom Line

Bank of America's checking lineup is structured around different balances, banking habits, and levels of account functionality.

**Advantage SafeBalance Banking** focuses on straightforward digital banking and does not charge Overdraft Item Fees. **Advantage Plus Banking** provides broader everyday functionality and a $12 monthly fee that can be waived through a $1,500 minimum daily balance, qualifying direct deposit, or applicable BofA Rewards status. **Advantage Relationship Banking** is an interest-bearing option with a $25 monthly fee and a substantially higher $20,000 minimum daily balance requirement for the standard fee waiver.

The right comparison therefore depends on how the account will actually be used. Looking at monthly fees alongside direct-deposit requirements, minimum balances, overdraft policies, interest, wire services, and relationship benefits provides a more complete picture of the potential cost of a Bank of America checking account.
